## 1. Purpose and scope

This document records Worexa Technologies' ownership and operating terms for the My Dear Partner web application, mobile-compatible web experience, APIs, software, content, branding, payments, support and associated services (together, the **Platform**).

It is an India-oriented operational draft. It must be checked against the entity's incorporation documents, tax registrations, payment-provider agreement, current law, and the jurisdictions in which the Platform is offered before it is published or relied upon.

## 2. Intellectual-property and project ownership

Unless a written agreement states otherwise, Worexa Technologies owns all right, title and interest in the Platform, including:

- source code, databases, schemas, APIs, deployment configuration and documentation;
- the My Dear Partner name, logo, domain names, visual identity, UI/UX design, text, graphics, photographs commissioned by Worexa Technologies, videos and marketing material;
- product concepts, workflows, matching logic, moderation processes, payment integrations and analytics configurations; and
- all improvements, updates, adaptations, derivatives and future versions of the Platform.

Third-party software, fonts, payment services, icons and other licensed materials remain subject to their respective licences. Worexa Technologies must maintain a software-bill-of-materials and licence register for all third-party dependencies.

No user, contractor, employee, vendor or partner receives any ownership interest in the Platform merely by using, accessing, contributing to or paying for the service. Any contractor or employee creating work for the Platform must sign a written IP-assignment and confidentiality agreement.

## 3. Limited user licence

Subject to these terms and a valid account, Worexa Technologies grants a personal, limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable and non-sublicensable licence to access and use the Platform only for lawful personal matchmaking purposes.

Users must not copy, scrape, sell, lease, reverse engineer, modify, distribute, create derivative works from, interfere with, bypass security for, or commercially exploit the Platform or another user's data without Worexa Technologies' written consent.

## 4. Eligibility and account rules

Users must:

- be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into a binding agreement;
- provide accurate and current registration information;
- use only one personal account unless Worexa Technologies authorises otherwise;
- keep login credentials confidential and promptly report suspected compromise;
- not impersonate another person or create a misleading, fraudulent or abusive profile; and
- comply with applicable law and these terms.

Worexa Technologies may request verification information, limit features, suspend or close an account where it reasonably believes a user has violated these terms, created risk for another user, or is required to do so by law.

## 5. Member content and profile information

Users retain ownership of content they submit, but grant Worexa Technologies a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to host, process, display and use that content only as necessary to operate, secure, improve and promote the Platform in accordance with the user's privacy settings and applicable law.

Users are responsible for the accuracy, lawfulness and permissions for submitted information, images and documents. Users must not upload unlawful, defamatory, hateful, sexually explicit, infringing, deceptive, malicious or privacy-invasive content.

Profile visibility, photographs, contact information and messages must be handled according to the Platform's privacy controls. The Platform must not reveal a user's private contact details to another member without an appropriate user action or lawful basis.

## 6. Safety and conduct

The Platform is intended to support respectful introductions. It does not guarantee the identity, background, intentions, compatibility, conduct or availability of any member.

Users must not harass, threaten, discriminate against, stalk, solicit money, conduct scams, request financial credentials, distribute malware, solicit unlawful activity or use the Platform for commercial advertising.

Members should communicate carefully, keep conversations on the Platform where practical, avoid sending money or confidential financial information, and report suspicious behaviour through the support or reporting tools.

Worexa Technologies may investigate reports, preserve relevant records where lawful, remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts and cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities.

## 7. Privacy and personal data

Worexa Technologies will process personal data only for identified, lawful purposes such as account creation, profile operation, matching, fraud prevention, verification, support, payments, legal compliance and service improvement.

The published Privacy Policy must state:

- the categories of data collected, including profile, contact, verification, payment and technical information;
- why the data is processed and the applicable consent or other lawful basis;
- visibility controls and how users can manage, correct, download or delete eligible information;
- retention periods or criteria;
- categories of processors and service providers, including payment, cloud, messaging and support vendors;
- security measures and incident-response contact;
- cross-border processing, if any; and
- grievance/contact details and the process for exercising privacy rights.

Verification documents, passwords, session tokens and payment credentials require heightened controls. Passwords must be stored only using strong one-way password hashing. Payment-card data must not be stored by the Platform unless the applicable payment-security requirements are met; use a compliant payment provider instead.

## 8. Memberships, payments, cancellation and refunds

The Platform must clearly display each plan's price, currency, duration, included features, renewal terms, taxes where applicable, payment method and cancellation/refund conditions before payment.

Payments are processed by the designated payment service provider. Worexa Technologies does not guarantee payment-provider availability and may require a successful payment confirmation before enabling paid benefits.

Unless a mandatory law or a separately published refund policy says otherwise:

- memberships are personal and non-transferable;
- benefits begin only after successful payment and activation;
- unused time or features are not automatically refundable;
- fraud, chargeback abuse, policy violations or duplicate accounts may result in suspension and review; and
- valid refund requests must be submitted through Support with the order reference and reason.

The final published Refund and Cancellation Policy must specify the actual refund window, eligibility criteria, review timeline, settlement method, and payment-provider limitations. Do not publish vague or misleading pricing, discounts, auto-renewal or refund claims.

## 9. Disclaimers and limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the Platform is provided on an “as is” and “as available” basis. Worexa Technologies does not warrant that the Platform will be uninterrupted, error-free, completely secure, or that any connection will result in a relationship or marriage.

Worexa Technologies is not responsible for information shared, transactions made, meetings arranged or conduct occurring outside the Platform. Nothing in this section limits liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Where permitted by law, Worexa Technologies' aggregate liability arising from a user's use of paid services is limited to the amount paid by that user to Worexa Technologies for the relevant service during the preceding 12 months.

## 10. Indemnity

Users agree to indemnify and hold harmless Worexa Technologies and its personnel from claims, losses, liabilities and reasonable expenses arising from their unlawful use of the Platform, breach of these terms, infringement of third-party rights, or submitted content, except to the extent caused by Worexa Technologies' own unlawful conduct.

## 11. Suspension, termination and data deletion

Users may request account closure through the Platform or Support, subject to verification and any lawful retention requirements. Worexa Technologies may retain information necessary for fraud prevention, legal claims, accounting, compliance, security, dispute resolution and enforcement.

Account closure does not erase records that must be retained by law or that have already been anonymised or aggregated. The Privacy Policy must state the actual retention periods and deletion process.
