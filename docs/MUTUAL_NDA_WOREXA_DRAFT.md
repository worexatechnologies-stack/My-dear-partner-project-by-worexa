## Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate, discuss or pursue a possible business relationship relating to the design, development, operation, commercialisation, investment, partnership, service delivery or support of the **My Dear Partner** matchmaking platform and related Worexa Technologies products and services (the **Purpose**).

## 1. Confidential Information

**Confidential Information** means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the **Discloser**) to the other Party (the **Recipient**), whether oral, written, visual, electronic or otherwise, that is marked confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential in light of its nature or the circumstances of disclosure. It includes:

- business plans, strategies, forecasts, pricing, financial information and customer information;
- software, source code, APIs, databases, security measures, product roadmaps, designs, specifications, algorithms, documentation and know-how;
- member, supplier, contractor and partner information;
- non-public marketing, research, commercial and operational information; and
- the existence and terms of this Agreement and the Parties' discussions.

## 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate:

1. is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;
2. was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure by the Discloser;
3. is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or
4. is independently developed without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. Recipient obligations

The Recipient shall:

- use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- protect it with at least reasonable care and no less care than it uses for its own similar confidential information;
- disclose it only to employees, contractors, professional advisers or affiliates who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;
- not copy, reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble or otherwise analyse software, prototypes or other materials except where expressly permitted in writing by the Discloser or required by applicable law; and
- promptly notify the Discloser of any actual or suspected unauthorised access, use or disclosure.

## 4. Compelled disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, promptly give the Discloser written notice and reasonable cooperation so that the Discloser may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Recipient shall disclose only the portion legally required.

## 5. Term and survival

This Agreement remains in effect for **two (2) years** from the Effective Date unless terminated earlier by either Party with **thirty (30) days'** written notice.

The confidentiality and restricted-use obligations survive termination for **five (5) years**. For trade secrets, the obligations continue for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 6. No licence or obligation

No licence, assignment, transfer or ownership right in Confidential Information, intellectual property or other materials is granted by this Agreement. Neither Party is obligated to enter into any further transaction, project or commercial relationship.

## 7. Ownership of Worexa Technologies materials

As between the Parties, Worexa Technologies retains all right, title and interest in the My Dear Partner project and its related source code, APIs, databases, documentation, branding, designs, product concepts, confidential business information and improvements, except as expressly agreed in a separate written agreement signed by an authorised representative of Worexa Technologies.

## 8. Return or destruction

On written request of the Discloser, the Recipient shall promptly return or securely destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and, if requested, certify destruction in writing. The Recipient may retain archival copies solely to comply with law, regulation, internal backup processes or professional obligations, provided the retained material remains protected under this Agreement.

## 9. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that unauthorised disclosure or use may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages may be inadequate. The non-breaching Party may seek injunctive or equitable relief in addition to any other remedies available under applicable law.

## 10. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Subject to mandatory law, the courts located in **Bengaluru, Karnataka, India** shall have exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising from this Agreement.

## 11. General terms

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions on confidentiality for the Purpose. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition or transfer of substantially all relevant assets. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by valid electronic signature.
